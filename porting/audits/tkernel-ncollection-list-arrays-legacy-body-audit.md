Two test-body discrepancies were corrected:

- `NCollection_ListTest.STLIterators` compared native `begin()` with `cbegin()` and `end()` with `cend()`. Rust compared the initial iterators and exhaustion, but did not compare the exhausted iterators. The test now drains both iterators and compares their end states.
