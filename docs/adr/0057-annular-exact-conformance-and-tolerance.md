# ADR0057: Exact annular conformance and stored tolerance policy

Status: implemented private integration candidate; no ordered body completion.
Scope: RustyCADKernel#10 prerequisite over ADR0052's sealed actual annular join.

## Decision

Consume only `AnnularFrozenDomain`. Bind every returned proof to its complete
replay, including source region proof, actual copy/charts, named domains, raw
vertex operands and unchanged operation limits. No caller-authored operand set,
mutable geometry recapture or invented single-loop contact identifier can issue
this proof. Earlier single-boundary exact conformance and tolerance behavior
remain selectable with unchanged callbacks and bytes.

First extract the existing projection frame, endpoint incidence, trim identity
and raw metadata drivers structurally. Keep their old wrappers and exact check
order. The concrete annular wrapper supplies its admitted census: twelve
canonical endpoint proofs cover twenty-four directed raw uses; four named faces
cover twelve directed trims; fourteen physical body entities retain metadata.
These counts are completeness checks over the already sealed correspondence,
not independent topology or domain certificates.

## Exact geometry and upstream closure

Admit the exact unscaled determinant of every used placement and its separate
finite nonzero scale, preserving the existing finite-origin policy. Never form
a rounded scale-times-matrix product to decide invertibility. Reuse the existing
raw placed endpoint residual engine with a separate exact-zero query. Reuse a
physical endpoint result only after every directed local point, actual placement
and canonical native parameter agree bit for bit.

Upstream two-loop proofs already retain exact zero residual reports for original,
census and actual candidate native closure endpoints. Their source-stage reports
also retain requested/effective policy and work. Consume these bound reports
without re-evaluating geometry, after admitting the associated raw point and
curve/support placement frames. Retain source, stage, candidate and authored wire
roles explicitly. The copied-base endpoints are linked to those exact source
operands by the sealed binding; retain that correspondence and admit its raw
frames as well. Zero world discrepancy alone does not authorize a singular
local frame or reinterpret a stored tolerance as world-space uncertainty.

Complete chart proofs and exact named trim correspondences supply zero trim
residuals. No tessellation, sampling, native rounded world point, approximate
inverse or widened request can discharge a required check. Every missing,
unsupported or inconclusive required proof remains typed incomplete.

## Separate metadata stage

The tolerance operation requires an already completed conformance proof bound
to the same full immutable join. It never repeats geometry work. Observe finite
nonnegative raw entity metadata once per physical definition and retain every
directed context, the compatibility accessor floor and the explicit cap decision.
Require explicit NoLimit, Report or Refuse policy. A Refuse exceedance retains all
observations and returns failure; Report is diagnostic under the selected policy.
Original operation spatial/parameter limits remain unchanged and separate from
exact-zero proof bounds and metadata caps. No vertex/edge/face tolerance hierarchy
is inferred.

## Result, cancellation and integration contract

Return private immutable Send+Sync evidence, full bound operands, requested and
effective settings, certified error provenance, and typed owned failures with
phase/entity/context and partial diagnostics. New operation-local progress is
Send. Every deterministic callback may cancel before completion; no partial
geometry or metadata report is promoted into successful body status.

Conformance is reusable at the geometry stage of a future ordered validator;
tolerance follows topology, geometry and self-intersection in that composition.
Neither helper marks an ordered stage passed by itself. The public operation
must rebind live inputs after its final callback. This immutable-only slice adds
no native capture pipeline and no concurrent mutation claim.

## Verification and migration

Pin the old complete replay corpus and exact callback phase trace before sharing
machinery, and compare complete bytes after the structural commit. Verify the
actual annulus census, canonical/directed endpoint associations, source-stage
closure provenance, both boundary roles, singular/rounding traps, negative scales,
cap modes and raw zero/subfloor values, missing/duplicate/foreign contexts,
complete cancellation prefixes and post-cancellation reuse. Preserve original
source refusals if they correctly occur before this stage; owned scalar fixtures
cover unreachable arithmetic counterexamples without weakening earlier gates.

Compare independent processes and concurrent reads/operations. Retain source
hashes, Rust 1.99.0, two build jobs and all executed commands. The existing host
ThreadSanitizer startup failure remains an unfulfilled instrumentation gate.
Within the Kernel#10 complete annular-operation milestone, route the ordered
consumer through these shared drivers and remove temporary duplicate adapters
only after corpus validation. Earlier behavior and bytes require an explicit
recorded migration before deletion.
