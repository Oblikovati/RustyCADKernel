## Explicit limitations

The evidence uses owned combinatorial expectations and actual existing Rust
builder observations, without a new OCCT execution or GPU use. Rational, split,
multi-edge and general Boolean histories remain incomplete. Historical copy
observations establish lineage, not fresh geometry validity. The assembler must
perform its final shared live join rebind and history-only reprojection with no
callback between or after them.

Successful history and owned findings have compiler-enforced Send/Sync and no
lock-taking read views. Owner-bearing legacy refusal payloads remain in the
separate operation-local envelope. Ordinary-host ThreadSanitizer acceptance
remains **unverified**: the earlier instrumented trivial control failed before
its main marker; exact toolchain/commands and exclusions are retained under
`../frozen-prism-projection-v1-2026-10-14/tsan-metadata.json`. No security or
runtime bypass was used. Deterministic stress is not sanitizer acceptance.
