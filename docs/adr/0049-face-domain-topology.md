# ADR0049: face domains in prism topology

Status: accepted design; production V2 domain issuance pending.

## Decision

Use one finite incidence implementation with an internal face-domain policy seam.
The legacy V1 policy retains its single-wire face admission, sphere Euler check,
callback positions and full replay bytes. Structural extraction is separate from
any extension of accepted topology domains. All existing fixtures remain owned
regression inputs.

A future V2 policy must be issued from the same actual plural prism join and
certified region. It binds complete joined and region bytes and an exact bijection
from actual face occurrences and directed wire occurrences to certified parameter
domains. Caller weights, genus labels and loop counts cannot issue this policy.
Missing or inconclusive domain evidence remains incomplete.

For each connected orientable genus-zero parameter domain with b certified
boundary components, its contribution is 2-b. The annular caps contribute zero;
the seam-cut rectangular side charts each contribute one, although their physical
images are cylindrical. The surface contribution is V-E+sum(face contributions).
The expected characteristic follows from twice the certified base-region
characteristic and actual product correspondence. It is not a caller integer.

Retain complete cyclic-wire, opposite-edge-use, cyclic-vertex-link and connected-
face checks. Preserve full use paths, shared seam multiplicity, source identities,
boundary orientations and containment. Aggregate Euler counts do not discharge
these obligations. These are exact combinatorial checks, not embeddedness,
outwardness, geometry consistency or successful-solid authority.

## Current scope and verification

The first structural prefix exposes only an internal seam. An independent annular
cell-word fixture may exercise it through a test-only policy. There is no
production V2 constructor or success entrypoint until the actual domain binding
exists. Symbolic fixtures cannot fabricate region or join certificates.

The independent fixture cuts each annular cap along one radial edge. Its finite
cell complex has four vertices, eight edges and four disk faces. Exact rational
boundary ranks are both three, giving Betti numbers (1,2,1) and characteristic
zero. Retained Jacobian results establish these matrix ranks only; they do not
certify the geometric production operation. Removing the two artificial cuts
leaves V=4, E=6, two annular caps and two disk charts, also characteristic zero.

A completed production extension must additionally reject missing/duplicate hole
bindings, swapped role/orientation, foreign source bytes, disconnected domains,
missing seam uses and pinched links. It must bind all parameter-domain proofs,
preserve earlier replays and pass the full operation corpus before publication.

## Migration and deletion

No production path is superseded by the structural seam. When V2 passes its
complete owned corpus, migrate the current operation during C1 and delete the
superseded production selection in the same milestone, retaining V1 replay as
required by the recorded behavior migration. No default changes in this prefix.
