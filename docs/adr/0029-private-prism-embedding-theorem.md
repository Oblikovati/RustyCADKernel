# Private trimmed-prism embedding theorem, behavior version 1

## Decision and dependency

Add an explicit opt-in, private inspection after the existing single-boundary
Jordan region and actual prism-chart pipeline. Its output is a source-bound
mathematical witness for the admitted trimmed domains and their allowed
intersections. It does not mutate `PrismReceipt`, promote completion, remove
mapped self-intersection gaps, or write `OrderedCoverage`.

The production entry depends on the cap-measurement work's committed, reviewed
shared fresh-binding helper. Reuse that helper's current region classification,
source/copy/closure checks, complete actual-face coverage, history parents,
chart/domain proofs and callback-free final rebind. Do not copy its work-in-progress
binder, introduce a second validator, or couple the theorem to scalar measures.
The helper must retain its fresh exact nonzero affine determinant, rather than
discarding that operand after a Boolean check.

The intended private contract is a borrowed inspection of the original bridge,
original sources and current prism receipt, explicit version-1 regularity options,
and caller cancellation. A successful `PrismEmbeddingEvidence` owns the fresh
binding operands, exact determinant, actual source regularity report, trimmed
domain declarations and all six self/pair rows below. Typed refusal retains the
original binding, numerical, incomplete or cancellation cause. Missing proof is
never a partial successful table. An unsupported version is invalid.

## Hypotheses and complete implication

The existing source-bound Jordan proof supplies one closed boundary
C:[0,1]→∂R with C(t)=C(u) only for t=u or {t,u}={0,1}, and the selected bounded
component R with winding +1 and complementary traversal -1. Its complete
self-census, zero source/copy residuals, closure contact and current candidate
bindings remain required. R denotes that captured bounded planar domain, not an
untrimmed support plane or a rectangle inferred from bounds.

For the exact current support placement A and requested extrusion E, retain

    F(u,v,s) = A(u,v) + s E,
    J = det(A_u,A_v,E) != 0.

The shared affine Jacobian operation proves F injective on all parameter triples.
Apply that implication only to the represented closed domain R̄×[0,1]. Its two
caps have domains R̄×{0} and R̄×{1}. The side has boundary parameter t∈[0,1]
and normalized sweep s∈[0,1], modulo exactly (0,s)~(1,s).

The current stored side chart has parameters (t,v), where v=-h*s and h>0 is the
retained height proved by the existing side trim/domain checks. The exact
side-direction identity is E=-h times the placed stored direction. Never assume
that the stored surface's second parameter equals normalized sweep parameter s.

Run `certify_bezier_regularity` on the fresh Generated chart's actual captured
source poles, optional weights, placement and full native interval. Reuse the
existing source/copy/chart identities to bind that report to the Jordan boundary
and the actual extrusion basis. An unrelated retained regularity report or a
retained success flag supplies no evidence. A stationary or everywhere-stationary
classification refuses this theorem. Preserve the distinct stored-degree cap,
reduced-degree limits, root work policy and complete denominator proof.

Nonzero boundary derivative together with transverse E proves regular side
interiors. J!=0 also proves regular cap interiors. The side's authored closure
may be a corner; neither global C1 closure nor injectivity of the unquotiented
side rectangle is asserted.

## Six exhaustive rows over actual trimmed domains

Every row names the actual captured face occurrence(s), full trimmed domain(s),
authored trim uses and every history parent used by the implication. The rows
cover the three admitted face self-domains and their three unordered pairs.
Their identities come from authored/history bindings, never list rank, proximity
or coordinate equality.

| Actual face domain(s) | Exact conclusion |
| --- | --- |
| First cap, R̄×{0}, with itself | Injective map of the planar domain |
| Last cap, R̄×{1}, with itself | Injective map of the planar domain |
| Generated side, [0,1]×[0,1], with itself | Injective modulo exactly the authored t=0/t=1 seam |
| First cap / Last cap | Disjoint |
| First cap / Generated side | Exactly their authored first-rim uses, (C(t),0) |
| Last cap / Generated side | Exactly their authored last-rim uses, (C(t),1) |

For side self-incidence, retain both Start/End directed uses, their source
closure-contact contexts and parent multisets, their full stored intervals and
the t=0/t=1 parameter association. For each cap/side pair retain both authored
rim uses and the exact parameter correspondence. Closure/rim endpoints belong
to these same domains; they do not create additional unaccounted intersections.
An extra/missing/ambiguous face or trim prevents a complete table.

The final callback-free rebind must compare all theorem-used operands, including
source/copy identities, closure contexts, support/basis/direction/placement,
height, pcurves, exact intervals and complete trim-expectation payloads. It must
not rely on scalar measurement equality or an old diagnostic status.

## Owned expectations and acceptance gates

The retained cubic controls (0,0),(4,0),(0,4),(0,0), with E=(0,0,2), give J=2,
actual stored height h=2, two distinct cap domains and one authored side seam.
Its derivative norm identity is `||C'||²=16+32(9t²-9t+2)²>=16`; ADR 0025 retains
the independent Jacobian identity receipt. Its endpoint tangents (12,0) and
(0,-12) are different: the expected accepted closure is a corner, not C1.

The owned quartic controls (0,0),(0,0),(4,0),(0,4),(0,0) give
x=24t²(1-t)² and y=16t³(1-t). On (0,1), y/x=(2/3)t/(1-t) proves interior
injectivity, but C'(0)=0. The exact derivative gcd is t, independently checked
with the retained Jacobian request/response in
`region_certificate/embedding_quartic_gcd.json`. It checks the supplied derivative
polynomials, not the Rust implementation. This fixture must remain a genuine
refusal; if the current census cannot admit it, preserve that refusal without
weakening census admission or fabricating a Jordan receipt.
The owned current-path baseline retains the
`ExhaustivePairAndSelfDomains` obligation for this quartic and all four region
obligations on the incomplete prism receipt. Its direct regularity witness is
therefore separate from admission by the current region pipeline.

Before behavior work, owned tests pin current cubic operands, actual rim/seam
uses, stationary quartic evidence and unchanged incomplete body/gap behavior.
The completed slice must also cover reversal/placement, altered source/copy,
callback mutation, forged retained statuses, changed parent/role/trim/range/height,
extra or missing faces, cancellation and exhausted/declined regularity. Existing
normalized-oblique chart mismatch stays refused. Serial/thread/process replay
must keep mathematical evidence separate from operation-local topology addresses;
persistent publication identity belongs to the authored-ID ownership work.

## Versioning and exclusions

Behavior version 1 is additive and private. Existing entry points and their
incomplete/gap semantics remain selectable unchanged. Structural extraction of
the shared binder must have unchanged owned baselines and a separate commit.
No workspace version migration or replacement engine is introduced. Future
incompatible behavior requires a new behavior version and the applicable semantic
version/migration decision; old behavior remains replayable until migration.

The theorem does not establish ordered body validation, sewing/topological
manifoldness, tolerance consistency, area/volume acceptance, ownership of the
mutable legacy body, application success or support-surface self-intersection
freedom outside the named trims. It cannot clear `PrismInjectivityAndSurfaceCharts`
or a mapped self-intersection status by itself. Publication requires all fresh
bindings and full ordered coverage under the declared body kind.

No tessellation, sampling, new native oracle, separate intersection engine,
shape-specific fallback, GPU work or broad completion credit is permitted.
ThreadSanitizer remains subject to the recorded host startup limitation; ordinary
Send/Sync and replay checks are not complete thread-safety acceptance.
