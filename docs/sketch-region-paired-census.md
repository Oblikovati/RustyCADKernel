# Source-bound pair and self census consumer

This private issue #10 / ADR0014 consumer uses the checked
`CapturedBezierPair` provider. It introduces no solver and changes no mapped
OCCT builder, intersector, classifier, or numerical constant. The actual
`enumerate_source_bound_census` boundary attaches the full paired evidence to
its owned `BoundaryCensus`; it is not an unused standalone certificate helper.
The existing source-bound callers now receive that attached evidence.

Every unordered occurrence pair and each canonical self domain remain present
in deterministic traversal order. The provider owns the actual controls,
optional rational weights, and captured interval. Each report retains its source
slots, query mode, requested policy, remaining effective policy and its actual proof variant. Graph proofs retain
complete root and sign evidence, paired-parameter graphs and outward world-error
construction; constant exclusions retain their exact source equation witness
without fabricated graph or sign execution.
Only complete reports lend certificates. Complete empty, invalid capture,
unsupported or inconclusive domains, numerical failure and cancellation remain
distinct. Same-physical cross-occurrence coincidence is explicitly incomplete;
a self off-diagonal proof cannot certify its removed diagonal or overlap.

Original occurrence IDs, location and orientation, captured representation ranges,
and full preserved/modified/generated parent rows remain separate from the
operation-local physical rank used for reuse. Reversed aliases do not cause a
second numerical evaluation. Reusing a failed or incomplete report retains its
original cause and budget prefix rather than retrying it with a new allowance.
No failed result becomes a completed contact certificate. A new operation may
retry with its own explicit policy; previous immutable reports remain unchanged.

All numerical pair executions share one requested completed-split budget. Cold
reports charge their actual work, including failed and cancelled stages. Reused
references charge zero new work. Source-conformance metric work keeps its own
separately declared policy and ledger; this is not a combined whole-operation
budget claim. Current acceptance rechecks the complete occurrence matrix,
source descriptors, full history kinds and parents, actual source-conformance
binding, policy and work ledger. Mutating a private snapshot, live geometry,
policy, history or gap vector cannot manufacture a current certificate.

The old `enumerate_boundary_census` and renamed
`enumerate_source_bound_line_diagnostic` preserve their existing one-axis native
fixtures and provisional endpoint inspection. The old inspector rejects paired
receipts explicitly; it does not treat the lack of legacy reports as certified
empty coverage. Its deletion task is issue #10: replace the diagnostic contact
path only after the paired actual-source/contact identity, dual parameter,
world construction and partition corpus passes. The migration limit is the
same R3 milestone as ADR0014, with removal before a complete public region
operation is accepted. Until then it remains a named diagnostic, not an
alternative production engine or success path.

Paired coverage alone does not establish mapped split correspondence, candidate
search, self-intersection freedom, tolerance consistency or healthy region/solid
completion. Exact contact brackets are never widened to include approximate
mapped split values. A contact still requires actual generated-parent/shared
vertex incidence, complete canonical identity and separately certified parameter
and world construction limits. Partition observations and parent membership do
not supply exhaustive search. Prism chart/injectivity, achieved error, composed
deletion coverage and application persistent naming remain separate obligations.

Empty source admission preserves `CensusCause::Input(ArrangementCause::Empty)`;
it does not create a completed healthy body or a vacuous domain certificate.
An admitted receipt whose occurrence universe has been removed retains missing
source and exhaustive-domain obligations even when no numerical report exists.
Exact constant exclusions retain genuine source equations and denominators;
they supply completed-empty self domains without fabricated graph certificates.
