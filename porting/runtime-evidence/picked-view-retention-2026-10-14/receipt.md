Pinned OCCT `3d097a0328e71b826377d4814ab05ec3c3d23871` AIS_ViewController::HandleViewEvents holds the actual local `aPickedView` through OnSubviewChanged and GLInput::Reset. The new Kernel capability moves the original handle into stable local storage and lends weak aliases to that slot. Retaining, cloning an alias, and projecting an alias add no intrusive target owner. The scope owner is fixed, local, and not Clone; return/unwind expires its aliases. Ordinary Handle/borrowed field references still work and reject lifetime erasure by default. Custom borrowed/thread-local implementations can delegate the explicit capability without new global bounds. Thread-safe retained fields and arbitrary mutable retained locals are outside this bounded prerequisite.
