# ADR0054: conditional orientation of one named annular prism

Status: private behavior V2 development; full integration gate pending.

## Decision and authority

`inspect_annular_orientation` consumes one immutable `AnnularFrozenDomain`,
whose existing issuer binds the actual two-loop region, copy/sweep charts,
complete four-face/ten-pair embedding table, authored contexts and frozen V2
projection. Raw caller rows cannot issue this proof. The result checks all four
face directions and twelve directed trim uses against the same joined input.
Both Generated faces remain distinguished by certified source boundary and full
occurrence ancestry. A cap rim is matched to its exact outer/inner wire ancestry,
never by its repeated First/Last role, position in a vector, or coordinates.

The existing V1 orientation path remains selectable and byte-identical. This
additive private V2 certificate does not change a frozen body's validity flag,
clear historical diagnostics, or publish a completed body. Ordered topology,
geometry/conformance, intersection, tolerance, history and final live-source
rebind remain separate required stages. This pure stage has no live native
capture, geometry evaluator, area/volume surrogate or new naming allocator.
No public export or package-version change is made by this private prerequisite.
A later public contract must record its supported domain and migration.

## Exact convention

The retained region winding query includes topology traversal. Let `w` be its
certified nonzero winding, and `r=-1` for a reversed source traversal, otherwise
`r=+1`. Increasing-parameter winding is `q=r*w`, not simply the field named
`native_signs` in the earlier region report. Both source roles must have certified
Inside winding +1 or -1. The pure `annular_boundary_directions` seam retains
source identity, w, traversal flag and q together. `m=+1` for the outer boundary
and `m=-1` for the inner boundary encodes the material side.

Let J be the exact sign of the retained affine prism Jacobian. Reusing the
unchanged V1 orientation helpers, required face signs are First=-J, Last=+J,
and boundary-side=-m*q*J. Cap trims have sign face*m*q. The side rectangle
uses the unchanged native-domain signs First/End=-1, Last/Start=+1 multiplied
by its face sign. These are conclusions about the certified trimmed charts and
native directed uses; they imply neither C1 closure at the authored seam nor
orientation of entire untrimmed supports.

## Independent expectations

The owned cubic has poles (0,0),(4,0),(0,4),(0,0). At t=1/2 its point is
(3/2,3/2), derivative (-3,3); the right-side vector (1,1) points outside the
outer region. Its half-scale translated inner copy has the opposite material
normal. Exact rational scalar triple products, independently evaluated with
Jacobian, are -6 for the outer positive sweep, +6 for the inner material side,
+6 after reflecting x, and +6 after stored-parameter reversal. Reversing the
sweep reverses this sign (the retained receipt also records a positive scalar
multiple +12). These local analytic checks determine sign expectations; the
existing source-bound embedding supplies the global domain theorem.

Actual pipeline fixtures exercise each stored-pole reversal independently,
each topology traversal reversal independently, their paired forms, reflection,
negative extrusion, and reflection plus negative extrusion. Topology reversal
changes w and r together and preserves q; stored reversal changes q. Literal
nominal face/trim tables independently distinguish outer and inner directions.
The default parameterized fixture compares full joined bytes with the original
entry. No sampled normal, measured volume, OCCT execution or native oracle is
used as expected-result evidence.

## Result, cancellation and replay

The owned certificate retains requested version, exact determinant sign, both
boundary conventions, full named face/trim rows, all parent classes and their
multiplicities, actual/required directions, and complete input bytes. Replay is
recomputed from these owned fields using explicit tags and binary64 bits; it
contains no process address. The new failure envelope keeps authored operation,
requested version, phase, full source binding, typed cause and derived class.
Missing/unsupported proof is incomplete; wrong actual directions are failed;
cancellation returns no partial certificate. Polling occurs at admission,
before each of four faces, and at commit. There is no callback after commit.
The outer operation still owns final callback-free live-source rebind.

Readers have no mutable source handle. Tests compare fresh rows and recomputed
bytes after source mutation and input drop, independent operations, and threaded
reads. Send/Sync assertions and ordinary concurrency tests are not a substitute
for the required release ThreadSanitizer acceptance; the previously observed
host startup limitation remains explicit. Final evidence will record exact
revisions, policies, source hashes, process byte comparisons and executed gates.
